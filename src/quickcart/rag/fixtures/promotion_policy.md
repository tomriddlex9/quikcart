# Promotion Policy

Rules for creating, stacking, and honouring promotions across QuickCart.

## Approval thresholds

Store agents may apply discounts up to 10% to resolve a service issue. The
duty manager may approve up to 30%. Any discount deeper than 30% requires
regional marketing sign-off before it is shown to a customer. Flash campaigns
and sitewide codes are created only by the central growth team; stores cannot
publish their own promo codes.

## Stacking and exclusions

One promo code may be applied per order. Auto-applied category discounts stack
with a promo code unless the promotion is explicitly marked exclusive. Alcohol,
pharmacy, and tobacco items are excluded from all promotions and cannot be
discounted under any approval tier. Gift-card purchases do not earn promotional
credit.

## Pricing errors

An obvious mispricing of 90% or more below list price may be cancelled before
dispatch, with the customer notified and a $3 apology voucher issued. If the
order has already been delivered, the price is honoured and the loss is logged
for the regional marketing review. Borderline cases between 50% and 90% off
list price go to the duty manager for a decision.
