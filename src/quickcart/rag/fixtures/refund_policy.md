# Refund Policy

This policy defines when QuickCart customers receive refunds, how refunds are
paid, and how partial refunds are calculated. Store agents must apply the
current version; superseded versions are kept only for audit.

## Refund eligibility

Customers are eligible for a full refund when perishable items arrive spoiled,
damaged, or past their expiry date, provided the claim is made within 24 hours
of delivery. Non-perishable items qualify for a full refund within 7 days if
unopened and unused. Items ordered incorrectly by the customer (wrong size or
flavour chosen at checkout) are not eligible unless the product is defective.

## Refund methods

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 3 to 5
business days. Customers may instead choose instant store credit; choosing
store credit adds a 10% goodwill bonus capped at $5 per order. Refunds to
original payment cannot be split across two payment methods.

## Partial refunds

When one or more items are missing from a multi-item order, refund the exact
line-item amount for each missing item. If the final order is more than 60
minutes late, the delivery fee is refunded in full on top of any item refunds.
Partial refunds always use the original payment method regardless of the
customer's store-credit preference.
