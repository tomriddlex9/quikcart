# Customer Complaint Policy

How QuickCart acknowledges, compensates, and escalates customer complaints.

## Response time targets

Complaints received in the app between 08:00 and 23:00 must be acknowledged
within 2 hours; outside those hours by 09:00 the next day. The resolution
target is 24 hours from acknowledgement. Food-safety complaints skip the
standard queue and reach a duty manager within 30 minutes at any hour.

## Compensation matrix

Delivery more than 30 minutes late: a delivery-fee voucher equal to the fee
paid. Spoiled or damaged items: full refund plus a $5 voucher. A customer with
three or more late deliveries in 14 days receives a $10 loyalty voucher with a
personalised apology. Safety incidents are never auto-compensated; they are
assessed case by case with the regional manager.

## Escalation tiers

Tier 1 is the store chat agent, who can resolve complaints and apply the
standard compensation matrix. Tier 2 is the duty manager, required when
compensation above $25 is requested or the complaint involves food safety.
Tier 3 is the regional manager, who owns legal threats, regulator contact, and
any complaint trending on social media. Every escalation is logged in the CRM
with tier, owner, and timestamps.
