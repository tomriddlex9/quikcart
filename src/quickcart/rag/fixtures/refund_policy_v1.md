# Refund Policy

This version is superseded by Refund Policy 2.1 (effective 2026-01-15). It is
retained for audit only and must not be applied to new claims.

## Legacy refund rules

These legacy refund rules are superseded by version 2.1 and must not be
applied to new claims. Under version 1.0 all refunds were issued as store
credit only; payout to the original payment method was not offered. Claims had
to be lodged within 48 hours of delivery. Partial refunds were not permitted:
any issue with an order voided the entire order and re-billed the correct
items.
