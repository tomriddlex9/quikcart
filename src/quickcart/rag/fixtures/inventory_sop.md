# Inventory SOP

Standard operating procedure for stock control in QuickCart dark stores: when
to restock, how to count, and how to rotate perishable goods.

## Reorder triggers and emergency restock

Each SKU carries a reorder point based on forecast demand and supplier lead
time. When on-hand stock reaches the reorder point, the system raises a
standard replenishment order. Emergency restock is triggered when projected
stock cover falls below 4 hours; the store must immediately escalate to the
regional inventory lead and arrange an off-cycle supplier run the same day.
Emergency restocks are logged with reason and approving lead for weekly review.

## Cycle counting

High-value SKUs (alcohol, electronics, baby formula) are cycle counted daily
as part of the opening checklist. All remaining SKUs follow an ABC rhythm:
A-class items are counted weekly, B-class monthly, and C-class quarterly. Every
count is recorded in the inventory app with the counter's staff ID; a variance
above 2% forces a same-day recount supervised by the duty manager.

## Expiry and FEFO

Perishable stock is rotated first-expired-first-out (FEFO); FIFO is not
acceptable for chilled or fresh categories. Items approaching 50% of remaining
shelf life are marked down automatically, and anything past its expiry date is
removed from sale and recorded as waste the same day. Weekly waste reports go
to the regional inventory lead every Monday.
