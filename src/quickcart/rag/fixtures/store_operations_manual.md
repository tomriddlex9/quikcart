# Store Operations Manual

The day-to-day operating standard for QuickCart dark stores: opening, outages,
and cash control.

## Opening checklist

The duty manager completes the opening checklist before the first order slot
and signs it off in the store log.

All staff clock in and confirm their shift role; anyone unwell or without
uniform goes home on sick leave. Cold-chain units are checked and must read at
or below 4 degrees Celsius — any warmer unit is quarantined and reported before
picking starts. Pickers verify tote bags, printers, and scales are ready, and
rider tablets are charged above 80%.

The POS float is set to exactly $200 in standard denominations and counted by
two people. The duty manager walks the store once to confirm aisles are clear,
signage is lit, and emergency exits are unobstructed. The signed checklist is
photographed and attached in the ops app by 07:30.

## Power or systems outage

When the store loses power or core systems go down during trading hours, shift
to the offline order manifest printed every 30 minutes and keep picking
manually. UPS battery has a 30 minute target; if the outage will outlast it,
contact the ops center immediately. Notify the ops center in any case if
systems are down for more than 15 minutes so customer messaging can be
switched on. Age-restricted orders require a manual ID check while offline, and
no alcohol order may be released without one.

## Cash handling and till discrepancies

Tills are counted by two people at every shift change and the count is logged
against the POS report. A variance of $10 or more triggers a full recount
supervised by the duty manager; if the variance stands, file an incident
report to the store lead the same day. Variances under $10 are logged with a
note. Cash above the $200 float is dropped into the safe hourly.
