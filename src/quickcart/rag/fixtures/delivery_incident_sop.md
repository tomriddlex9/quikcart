# Delivery Incident SOP

Procedures for incidents that happen between pick-up and drop-off: rider
accidents, lost orders, and unreachable customers.

## Rider accident or injury

Rider safety comes before the order. Call emergency services first if anyone
is hurt and do not move an injured rider. The store reassigns the open order to
the next available rider immediately so the customer is not left waiting. The
duty manager logs the incident in the safety system within 1 hour, including
time, location, and witness contacts. Our insurance liaison handles all
follow-up communication with the rider; store staff must never discuss fault.

## Lost or never-arrived orders

When a customer reports an order never arrived, trace the rider GPS trail and
confirm the drop-off photo or PIN entry. If delivery cannot be verified,
contact the customer within 15 minutes, offer an immediate redispatch or a full
refund to the original payment method, and file a courier replacement claim the
same day. Two verified losses with the same rider in one shift trigger a rider
review by the fleet team.

## Customer unreachable at drop-off

Attempt to contact the customer three times, 5 minutes apart, by app message
then phone call. If the customer opted in to leave-at-door delivery and the
location is safe, leave the order with a photo. Otherwise return the order to
the store; chilled items go straight back to the shelf and the customer is
refunded minus no fee on the first occurrence. Note the attempt times in the
order log for every occurrence.
